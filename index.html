HOW TO PLAY

Name a published musical artist whose name begins with the last letter of the previous artist. No repeats. Ignore The at the beginning of a name.

SCORING · Every valid artist is +1 point. If an artist ends in S, you may escape to the letter before the S for −2 points. Pass for −3.
