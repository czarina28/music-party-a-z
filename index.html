THE ARTIST NAME GAME

Music Party A–Z

HOW TO PLAY

Name a published musical artist whose name begins with the last letter of the previous artist. No repeats. Ignore The at the beginning of a name.

SCORING · Every valid artist is +1 point. If an artist ends in S, you may escape to the letter before the S for −2 points. Pass for −3.

RULES OF THE GAME
  1. Name a musical artist or act.
  2. Your artist must begin with the final letter of the previous artist.
  3. Ignore The at the beginning of an artist name. The Supremes plays as S.
  4. An artist or act may only be used once in a game.
  5. Artists must be published — they must have an officially released recording.
  6. Every valid artist you play earns 1 point.
  7. S Escape: if the previous artist ends in S, you may use the letter immediately before the S instead. S Escape costs 2 points.
  8. Pass: if you're stuck, you may pass for −3 points. The computer plays from the current letter and the game continues.
