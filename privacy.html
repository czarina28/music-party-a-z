Music Party A–Z Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Music Party A–Z is designed to be played without an account and without providing personal information to the developer.

Data Collection

Music Party A–Z does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs and does not require an account.

Game Data

Gameplay uses the artist data packaged with the app. The app does not transmit your game activity to the developer.

Dictation

If you choose to use the iPhone keyboard’s dictation feature when entering an artist name, that feature is provided and controlled by Apple and is subject to Apple’s privacy practices. Music Party A–Z does not receive audio recordings from keyboard dictation.

Children’s Privacy

Because Music Party A–Z does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the developer through the support information provided on the Music Party A–Z App Store listing.
